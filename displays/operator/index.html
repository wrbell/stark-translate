          When something goes wrong
          
      
          
        Start is greyed out
        
          Look at the list under Is everything ready? on the Prepare tab. Red items must be fixed before starting; yellow items are warnings. Click Check again after fixing something. If the page says it is not connected to the operator service, the service itself needs restarting.
          
      
          
      
          
        The page says "Needs attention"
        
          The error text is shown at the top of the page. Click Stop and reset, wait until the status says Ready, then start again. If it happens twice, copy the error text and send it to the setup owner, or build a bundle below.
          
      
          
      
          
        The page says "Not connected"
        
          The browser has lost contact with the operator service on this computer. Captions may still be running. Wait a few seconds; if it does not recover, refresh this page. If it still says not connected, the operator service has stopped and the setup owner needs to restart it.
          
      
          
      
          
        The microphone was unplugged
        
          Plug it back into the same port. A notice appears when the list of audio devices changes. Check the microphone choice on the Prepare tab, then Stop captions and Start captions again.
          
      
          
      
          
        No captions appear, or the audience display says "Reconnecting"
        
          Refresh the audience display's browser tab first. If captions still do not appear within a minute of speaking, stop and start captions. Check that the right microphone is selected and that the speaker is close to it.
          
      
          
      
          
        Phones cannot open the link
        
          Phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer, and the link must use the computer's network address rather than "localhost". The audience display can also show its own QR code: click its header.
          
      
          
      
          
        Captions are slow or fall behind
        
          Close other programs on this computer, especially anything using the graphics card, then stop and start captions. Slower first captions right after starting are normal while the language models load.
          
      
          
      
          The full operator runbook lives in the project folder at docs/operator_runbook.md. A quick check that the service is alive: open /healthz on this computer's operator address.
